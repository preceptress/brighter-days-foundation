Our promise

Cancer can interrupt a childhood. It should never erase one.

Children deserve more than treatment. They deserve friends, confidence, support, play, therapy, and a place where they do not have to explain what they are going through.

Why we exist

Born from one family’s hardest chapter.

In 2023, our founder was diagnosed with a malignant brain tumor. What followed was surgery, treatment, endless appointments, therapy, fear, and a kind of isolation that no child should have to carry.

Today, she is herself again — laughing, learning, dreaming, and determined to make the road less lonely for the kids coming after her.

Brighter Days Foundation was created to help children facing similar diagnoses access therapeutic support, build friendships, and find a community that truly understands.

The heart of Brighter Days Foundation

“You get it” can be the most powerful sentence in the world.

Illness can make kids feel different from everyone around them. We want to create spaces where nobody has to explain the scars, the appointments, the fear, the fatigue, or the complicated feelings that come afterward.

This is a place for friendship, creativity, confidence, joy — and simply getting to be a kid.
